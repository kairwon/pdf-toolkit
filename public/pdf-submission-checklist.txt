PDF SUBMISSION CHECKLIST — LAB OF PDF
https://labofpdf.com/guides/pdf-submission-checklist

[ ] Confirm the official file type, size, page, naming, and deadline rules.
[ ] Keep an untouched original PDF.
[ ] Open the exact final downloaded file in a separate PDF reader.
[ ] Check the first, middle, and last pages for order, rotation, and omissions.
[ ] Inspect small text, equations, tables, diagrams, stamps, signatures, and QR codes.
[ ] Test searchable text when the recipient requires it.
[ ] Measure the final file and aim slightly below the upload limit.
[ ] Apply the recipient's required filename.
[ ] Recheck digital signatures, forms, and interactive features after editing.
[ ] Upload early enough to confirm the portal accepted the correct file.

The receiving organization's current instructions always take priority.
This checklist is a general verification aid and does not certify acceptance.
